My name is Raj Reddy, and I bring more than ten years of hands-on experience in the payments domain. Over that time I have worked across the systems and standards that keep card transactions moving safely from the terminal to the issuer and back. My expertise includes EMV chip processing, ISO 8583 message flows, IBM z/TPF, BASE24 Classic, and Hardware Security Modules (HSMs). I am comfortable in environments where reliability, security, and precise message handling are non-negotiable, and I enjoy turning complex switching and authorisation problems into solutions that operations teams can trust.

I hold a postgraduate qualification in Computing, which gave me a strong foundation in software, systems, and how large platforms are designed and operated. That background continues to shape how I approach payments work: I look at both the business outcome and the technical path, whether that means tracing an ISO 8583 field, validating an EMV cryptogram, or working with cryptographic keys on an HSM. I have spent a large part of my career around mission-critical stacks such as IBM z/TPF and BASE24 Classic, where uptime, auditability, and careful change control matter as much as feature delivery.

Away from work, family comes first. I am married and we have two daughters who keep me busy in the best possible way—school runs, homework, weekend chaos, and the kind of energy that makes a quiet evening feel like a rare win. Being a husband and a dad keeps me grounded, reminds me not to take myself too seriously, and gives every long day at the terminal a clearer purpose.

When I do get a spare hour, I stay active. Basketball and badminton keep me moving, while pool and table tennis are a different kind of focus—timing, patience, and a bit of friendly competition. Gaming is another hobby I come back to regularly; it is a way to unwind and also a reminder that good systems, like good games, reward clear rules and well-designed feedback.

At home I also share the space with two cats: a British Shorthair and a Scottish Fold. Between my wife, the girls, the cats, and a career in high-volume payments, life is rarely dull. In short, I am a payments specialist who cares about secure, dependable card processing—and a husband and dad who balances that work with family, sport, games, and two very distinct feline colleagues.
